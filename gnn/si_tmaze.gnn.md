Full TMaze Sophisticated Inference Validation

## ModelAnnotation

The canonical pymdp T-maze anchor: a 5-location corridor with a cue, a reward
location, and a binary outcome, run under the `sophisticated_inference` planner
with horizon 5 and rollout length 6 (see `../pymdp.yaml`). The model is
dynamic: `q_pi` is the policy posterior over the 5 enumerated policies,
`first_action_prob` is its first-action marginal, and `si_tree_nodes` records
the exhaustive search tree the planner actually expanded. Numeric values are
generated by `../scripts/simulate_si_tmaze.py` into the T-maze model-matrix
audit rather than hand-authored here.
